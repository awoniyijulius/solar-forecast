# 📄 SolarSight: Project Blueprint & Investor Brief

## 1. Executive Vision
**SolarSight** is a "Resilience-First" renewable energy forecasting engine. While traditional platforms focus on centralized grid efficiency, SolarSight is built for the **decentralized future**. We empower individual homeowners, farmers, and microgrid operators to optimize solar yield, displace fossil-fuel backups, and achieve true energy autonomy.

**Mission**: To eliminate the "Intelligence Gap" in the global energy transition.

---

## 2. The Market Opportunity
Energy transition is the largest capital reallocation in human history. However, it faces two critical barriers:
1.  **Intermittency (Global South)**: In regions like Nigeria and Kenya, backup diesel generators account for 40%+ of energy costs. Solar is available but remains under-optimized without predictive intelligence.
2.  **Volatility (Developed Markets)**: High-cost peak hours in Europe and the US make battery management a complex financial gamble for households.

**SolarSight bridges this gap** by providing a unified API layer that translates raw weather data into actionable human and financial metrics.
